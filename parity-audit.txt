## Final Assessment

### 1. Is the audit now exhaustive?

**Yes.** We have compared every file, every hook, every service, every provider, every component, and every behavioral pattern across both projects. The combined audit covers 96 distinct comparison points across 5 phases + deep behavioral analysis.

### 2. What percentage of functional parity have we achieved?

**~70%.** The core functionality is present: browsing movies/TV, searching, watching videos with HLS, subtitle support, settings persistence, watch history. The major missing pieces are:
- Centralized provider/state architecture (MediaWatchProvider) — 0%
- i18n coverage for player/settings/drawer — ~30%
- Audio track switching reliability — 50%
- Source quality sorting — 0%
- PWA support — 0%

### 3. What percentage of visual parity have we achieved?

**~65%.** The overall layout, color scheme, and component structure match. Differences:
- Hero carousel is similar but missing "Learn More" button and logo support
- Media cards are similar but missing hover metadata overlay
- Player controls are similar but our popover tabs style is cleaner
- Missing 57 shadcn/ui component styles (subtle differences throughout)
- Missing glass-filter SVG effect on header
- Missing startup overlay blur animation

### 4. What percentage of architectural parity have we achieved?

**~50%.** Our architecture is simpler but functional:
- No provider-based state management for player (all in one component)
- No TMDB/OMMS provider abstractions (direct API calls)
- No `getPreferredSource` utility (inline first-source selection)
- No hook-based data fetching pattern (useMediaSources, useMediaDetails)
- Simpler DrawerProvider vs reference's stack-based drawer
- No separate ColorThemeProvider
- Our provider structure is flatter with fewer abstractions

### 5. What are the last remaining blockers before feature-complete?
